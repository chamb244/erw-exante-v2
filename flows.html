Ex-Ante ERW Pipeline

erw- Modelling approach

Per-pixel geospatial profitability of basalt enhanced rock weathering at ~1 km² across 47 Sub-Saharan African countries and 23 SPAM crops. Nine methodological blocks compose into a single decomposed-cost accounting (erw-7), with supply-constrained deployment ranking and five sensitivity sweeps on top. Feedstock chemistry, grain size, and allocation rule are exposed as decision variables; three temporal regimes — year-1, NPV @ 10%, and equilibrium — run in parallel.

B1 · WORKING GRID
SoilGrids + SPAM harmonisation

SSA mask from GADM v4; depth-weighted SoilGrids properties (pH, bulk density, exchangeable acidity, K/Ca/Mg/Na bases) co-registered with SPAM v2 harvested area, production, and yield for 23 crops at a common 10×-resampled grid. ECEC and acidity-saturation surfaces are derived inline.

erw-1 · erw-2
B2 · BASALT RATE
Lime demand × feedstock chemistry

Four lime-equivalent CaCO₃ rates (Kamprath, Cochrane, Aramburu-Merlos LiTAS year-1, maintenance) via limer::limeRate, converted to t basalt ha⁻¹ using user-set CaO/MgO mass fractions (default 10% / 7%), a Lewis-2021-calibrated reactive fraction, and a Strefler-2018 grain-size lever (10–500 µm). Uniform 10 / 20 / 50 t ha⁻¹ counterfactuals run in parallel.

erw-3
B3 · CDR SURFACE
Climate × pH × pedogenic-C deduction

Per-tonne CDR potential from CaO/MgO stoichiometry, scaled by a temperature×moisture climate factor (anchored to Tref=11 °C, Pref=1000 mm) and a triangular pH term peaked near pH 5. An aridity-binned pedogenic-carbonate fraction (UNEP 1992 bands on MAP) removes alkalinity that precipitates locally rather than exporting to the ocean.

erw-9
B4 · LOGISTICS
Delivered basalt-price surface

Basalt sources from GLiM v1.0 (basic-volcanic lithology). Cost-distance over the Malaria Atlas Project 2019 friction surface, monetised at $0.04 min⁻¹ t⁻¹ (30-t truck @ $80/h). Quarry-gate fines at $10/t; haul capped at 1000 km beyond which truck-diesel LCA would erase the credit.

erw-basalt-access
B5 · ENERGY
Country-resolved electricity

Per-country industrial electricity price ($/kWh) and grid carbon intensity (kg CO₂/kWh) rasterised onto the working grid. Drives both the grinding-cost term and the grinding-LCA term inside erw-7. Falls back to flat $0.10/kWh + 0.60 kg/kWh when missing.

erw-energy-country
B6 · AGRONOMIC RESPONSE
EcoCrop curves + Bayesian uplift

Per-crop EcoCrop pH and acidity-saturation tolerance tables (23 crops × 2 responses) feed a Recocrop relative-yield surface clamped to [0.2, 1]. In parallel, a Bayesian hierarchical brms regression on the curated ERW-trial dataset yields posterior uplift surfaces in pH, MAT, MAP, grain size, and crop type. erw-7 prefers the Bayesian uplift when present; otherwise falls back to EcoCrop.

erw-4 · erw-5 · erw-yield-fit · erw-yield-predict
B7 · PROFITABILITY
Decomposed cost + derived LCA

Cost side: quarry + grinding + transport + spreading. Credit side: CDR × $150/tCO₂ − $30/tCO₂ MRV. Yield side: agronomic uplift × FAOSTAT 2016–20 producer prices. Three regimes (year-1, NPV @ 10%, equilibrium) with a 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 / 10 % CDR phasing over five years.

erw-7
B8 · DEPLOYMENT FRONTIER
Supply-constrained ranking

Pixels ranked descending by gross margin per tonne basalt — the LP dual on the basalt-supply constraint. Produces supply curves and deployment masks at 1 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 Mt yr⁻¹ caps, plus an unconstrained reference.

erw-supply
B9 · SENSITIVITY
Five-axis robustness scan

(i) crop × basalt × carbon-price grid; (ii) yield uplift 1.0–2.5×; (iii) MRV 0–80 $/tCO₂; (iv) grain size 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 µm; (v) allocation rule (targeted + uniform 10 / 20 / 50). All sweeps run across the three regimes and 23 crops.

erw-8
Decision variables (and where they enter)
  • CaO_frac, MgO_frac — feedstock chemistry, B2 + B3
  • grain_size_um — default 50 µm, 10–500 µm, B2 + B9
  • allocation — LiTAS-targeted vs uniform 10 / 20 / 50, B7 + B9
  • CARBON_PRICE_USD_T — default $150/tCO₂, B7 + B9
  • DISCOUNT_RATE_PCT — default 10 %, NPV regime in B7
  • MRV_COST_USD_T_CO2 — default $20/tCO₂, B7 + B9
1. Conceptual framework — the scientific argument
Three causal streams converge on a per-pixel gross margin π(x,y) = RCDR + Ragro − C: a biogeochemical chain from feedstock chemistry and grain size through climate-and-pH dissolution kinetics to net alkalinity export; an agronomic chain from soil acidity through lime-equivalent demand and crop response to a yield-revenue term; and an economic-geography chain from feedstock supply through logistics, grinding, and operations to a total cost. The dashed cross-link captures the structural insight that agronomic lime demand sets the basalt application rate that the biogeochemistry stream then dissolves. Decision variables (top, dashed) modulate the whole framework; outputs (bottom) are the three deliverables.

2. Pipeline architecture — how the code implements it
Data → working grid → six geospatial surfaces → erw-7 profitability convergence → ranking and sensitivity. Optional arms (erw-basalt-access, erw-energy-country, erw-yield-predict) feed erw-7 when present; otherwise the pipeline falls back to flat constants and EcoCrop curves.

4. Profitability outcomes — gross margin under different assumptions

Rendered by erw/erw-10-profitability-maps-tables.R from the 276 per-crop gross-margin rasters in data/economics_erw/. Each map below is the crop-weighted GM (\$/ha) at the 0.083° pixel — weighted by SPAM harvested area across the 23 crops sharing that cell. Three return regimes (year-1, NPV @ 10%, equilibrium) × four allocation rules (targeted, uniform 10/20/50 t·ha−¹) span the assumption space. The companion CSV tables under docs/tables/ aggregate the same rasters by country and by crop — see also docs/erw-results.pdf for the full panel.
