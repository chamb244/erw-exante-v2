# Ex-Ante ERW Pipeline

**Geospatial ex-ante profitability model for Enhanced Rock Weathering (ERW) with basalt across Sub-Saharan Africa.**

Per-pixel (~1 km²) profitability of basalt-ERW across **47 SSA countries × 23 SPAM crops**, decomposed into a CDR credit term, an agronomic-yield-uplift term, and a delivered-cost term. Built on SoilGrids soil properties, SPAM v2 crop layers, EcoCrop response curves, GLiM basalt sources, the Malaria Atlas Project friction surface, WorldClim climate, country-level energy data, and FAOSTAT 2016–20 producer prices. Three temporal regimes — year-1, NPV @ 10 %, and equilibrium — run in parallel.

The model is structured as **nine methodological blocks** that compose into a single decomposed-cost accounting (`erw-7`), with a supply-constrained deployment ranking (`erw-supply`) and five sensitivity sweeps (`erw-8`) on top. Feedstock chemistry, grain size, allocation rule, carbon price, MRV cost, and discount rate are all exposed as decision variables.

## 2. Modelling approach — the nine blocks

| Block | Title | Stages | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | Working grid | `erw-1` · `erw-2` | SSA mask from GADM v4; depth-weighted SoilGrids properties (pH, bulk density, exchangeable acidity, K/Ca/Mg/Na bases) co-registered with SPAM v2 harvested area / production / yield for 23 crops at a common 10×-resampled grid. ECEC and acidity-saturation surfaces derived inline. |
| **B2** | Basalt rate | `erw-3` | Four lime-equivalent CaCO₃ rates (Kamprath, Cochrane, Aramburu-Merlos LiTAS year-1, maintenance) via `limer::limeRate`, converted to t basalt ha⁻¹ using user-set CaO / MgO mass fractions (default 10 % / 7 %), a Lewis-2021-calibrated reactive fraction, and a Strefler-2018 grain-size lever (10–500 µm). Uniform 10 / 20 / 50 t ha⁻¹ counterfactuals run in parallel. |
| **B3** | CDR surface | `erw-9` | Per-tonne CDR potential from CaO/MgO stoichiometry, scaled by a temperature × moisture climate factor (anchored at T<sub>ref</sub>=11 °C, P<sub>ref</sub>=1000 mm) and a triangular pH term peaked near pH 5. An aridity-binned pedogenic-carbonate fraction (UNEP 1992 bands on MAP) removes alkalinity that precipitates locally rather than exporting to the ocean. |
| **B4** | Logistics | `erw-basalt-access` | Basalt sources from GLiM v1.0 (basic-volcanic + basic-plutonic lithologies; ultramafics intentionally excluded for cropland use). Cost-distance over the MAP 2019 friction surface monetised at $0.04 min⁻¹ t⁻¹ (30-t truck @ $80/h). Quarry-gate fines at $10/t; haul capped at 1000 km. |
| **B5** | Energy | `erw-energy-country` | Per-country industrial electricity price ($/kWh) and grid carbon intensity (kg CO₂/kWh) rasterised onto the working grid. Drives both the grinding-cost term and the grinding-LCA term inside `erw-7`. Falls back to $0.10/kWh and 0.60 kg/kWh when missing. |
| **B6** | Agronomic response | `erw-4` · `erw-5` · `erw-yield-fit` · `erw-yield-predict` | Per-crop EcoCrop pH and acidity-saturation tolerance tables (23 crops × 2 responses) feed a `Recocrop` relative-yield surface clamped to [0.2, 1]. In parallel, a Bayesian hierarchical `brms` regression on the ERW-trial dataset yields posterior uplift surfaces. `erw-7` prefers the Bayesian uplift when present; otherwise falls back to EcoCrop. |
| **B7** | Profitability | `erw-7` | Cost side: quarry + grinding + transport + spreading. Credit side: CDR × $150/tCO₂ − $30/tCO₂ MRV. Yield side: agronomic uplift × FAOSTAT 2016–20 producer prices. Three regimes (year-1, NPV @ 10 %, equilibrium) with a 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 / 10 % CDR phasing over five years. |
| **B8** | Deployment frontier | `erw-supply` | Pixels ranked descending by gross margin per tonne basalt — the LP dual on the basalt-supply constraint. Produces supply curves and deployment masks at 1 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 Mt yr⁻¹ caps plus an unconstrained reference. |
| **B9** | Sensitivity | `erw-8` | Five-axis robustness scan: (i) crop × basalt × carbon-price grid; (ii) yield uplift 1.0–2.5×; (iii) MRV 0–80 $/tCO₂; (iv) grain size 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 µm; (v) allocation rule (targeted + uniform 10 / 20 / 50). All sweeps run across the three regimes and 23 crops. |

### Decision variables (and where they enter)

| Variable | Default | Enters at |
|---|---|---|
| `CaO_frac`, `MgO_frac` | 0.10 / 0.07 | B2 + B3 |
| `grain_size_um` | 100 µm | B2 + B9 |
| `allocation` | LiTAS-targeted | B7 + B9 |
| `CARBON_PRICE_USD_T` | $150/tCO₂ | B7 + B9 |
| `DISCOUNT_RATE_PCT` | 10 % | B7 (NPV regime) |
| `MRV_COST_USD_T_CO2` | $30/tCO₂ | B7 + B9 |

## 3. Pipeline — stages and run order

```
erw-0 → erw-1 → erw-2 → erw-3 → erw-4 → erw-5
                          │
                          ▼
                       erw-9   (CDR yield: climate + pedogenic carbonate)
                          │
            ┌─────────────┼──────────────────────┐
            ▼             ▼                      ▼
  erw-basalt-access  erw-energy-country   (erw-yield-fit → erw-yield-predict, optional)
            │             │                      │
            └─────────────┼──────────────────────┘
                          ▼
                       erw-7   (profitability — decomposed costs + time-resolved CDR)
                          │
            ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
            ▼             ▼             ▼
         erw-8       erw-supply    (headline figures)
      (sensitivity) (supply curves)
```

`erw-basalt-access`, `erw-energy-country`, and `erw-yield-predict` are optional sibling inputs — `erw-7` auto-detects each and falls back to flat constants or EcoCrop yield-loss when their output rasters are missing.

## 7. Running the pipeline

### Prerequisites

- **R ≥ 4.2** with the packages installed by `erw/erw-0-install-packages.R` (terra, geodata, **here**, limer, Recocrop, brms, malariaAtlas, readxl, reshape2, viridis).
- **LaTeX** (TeX Live or MacTeX) with `pdflatex` for the TikZ figures, and **pandoc + xelatex** for the document builds.
- **pdftocairo** (from poppler) for PDF → SVG / PNG conversion.

### Paths

All scripts resolve paths through the [`here`](https://here.r-lib.org) package, anchored by the empty **`.here`** marker file at the repo root:

```r
library(here)
input_path  <- paste0(here::here('data'), '/')   # everything reads from data/
output_path <- paste0(here::here('data'), '/')   # everything writes to data/
```

This means scripts work the same regardless of which directory you launch R from — there is **nothing to edit per machine**. Just clone the repo, install packages, and run.

### Run order

```bash
Rscript erw/erw-0-install-packages.R
Rscript erw/erw-1-layers-soilgrids.R
Rscript erw/erw-2-layers-spam.R
Rscript erw/erw-3-basalt-requirements.R
Rscript erw/erw-4-crop-parameters.R
Rscript erw/erw-5-crop-yield-loss-function.R
Rscript erw/erw-9-cdr-yield.R
# optional arms — wire in if data is available
Rscript erw/erw-build-basalt-mask.R
Rscript erw/erw-fetch-friction-surface.R
Rscript erw/erw-basalt-access.R
Rscript erw/erw-energy-country.R
Rscript erw/erw-yield-fit.R
Rscript erw/erw-yield-predict.R
# core profitability + downstream
Rscript erw/erw-7-profitability-function.R
Rscript erw/erw-supply.R
Rscript erw/erw-8-sensitivity-analysis.R
# figures for the docs
Rscript erw/erw-plot-maps.R
```

### Data dependencies

Scripts are host-independent (paths resolve via `here::here()`), but they still need the upstream data. Most layers are fetched programmatically by `geodata::*` and `malariaAtlas` on first run and cached under `data/`. Two layers require **manual download**:

- **GLiM v1.0 lithology** — place `LiMW_GIS 2015.gdb/` under `data/` (or the v1.0 shapefile under `data/access/glim/`). DOI: [10.1594/PANGAEA.788537](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.788537).
- **FAOSTAT producer prices** — drop `FAOSTAT_data_en_4-24-2023.csv` into `data/`.

See [`erw/erw-basalt-access.R`](erw/erw-basalt-access.R) for the GLiM loading logic and `data/` for which intermediates are already committed.

## 8. Current limitations

From [`docs/erw-README.md`](docs/erw-README.md) §"What this pipeline still does *not* do":

- **Bayesian yield response.** Wired up (`erw-yield-fit` → `erw-yield-predict`) but the trial dataset is small (~14 trials); the framework is prior-dominated and grows as new ERW field trials are appended. `erw-7` falls back to EcoCrop tolerance curves when uplift rasters are absent.
- **CGIAR-CSI Global Aridity Index.** The pedogenic-carbonate deduction in `erw-9` uses MAP alone as a proxy for AI = P/PET. Upgrade target: 1 km global aridity dataset.
- **Compaction emissions and N₂O.** Heavy basalt traffic on cropland causes some compaction; tilling-in basalt fines may briefly elevate N₂O. Neither is modelled.
- **Country-level credit eligibility / Article 6.** Some SSA countries restrict export of carbon credits or have unresolved corresponding-adjustment rules.
- **Tenure and aggregator finance structure.** Who pays the upfront ~$600/ha basalt cost, who owns the credit, and what aggregator structure makes MRV tractable for smallholders is unmodelled.
- **Industrial by-products** (steel slag, cement-kiln dust, mine tailings) are *not* represented and would shift the supply geography materially.
